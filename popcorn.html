Masala Popcorn

Ingredients

  • 2 cups popcorn
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon chaat masala
  • Salt as needed
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper powder

Instructions

  1. Heat butter in a pan on low flame.
  2. Add the prepared popcorn to the pan.
  3. Sprinkle chilli powder, turmeric powder, chaat masala, black pepper and salt.
  4. Mix everything well so that the spices coat the popcorn.
  5. Cook for 1–2 minutes on low flame.
  6. Turn off the flame and allow the popcorn to cool slightly.
  7. Serve the spicy Indian masala popcorn immediately.

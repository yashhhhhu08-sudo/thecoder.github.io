curd rice

Ingredients

  • 1-cup cooked rice
  • 1-cup curd
  • 1/2-cup milk
  • salt-as needed
  • 1-teaspoon oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1-teaspoon urad dal
  • 1-green chill,chopped
  • curry leaves
  • 1-tablespoon chopped coriander

Instructions

  1. cook the rice and allow it to cool
  2. mash the rice lightly
  3. add curd,milk,and salt,mix well
  4. heat oil in a pan
  5. add mustard seeds and let them splutter
  6. add urad dal,green chilli,and curry leaves.fry for a few second
  7. pour the tempering over the curd rice and mix well
  8. garnish with coriander and serve

MoongDal

Ingredients

  • Moong dal - 1 cup
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Grated carrot - ½ cup
  • Lemon juice - 1 tablespoon
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Roasted peanuts - ½ cup
  • Sev - ½ cup

Instructions

  1. Wash the moong dal properly with water.
  2. Soak the moong dal in water for 2 to 3 hours.
  3. Drain the water completely from the soaked dal.
  4. Chop the onion, tomato and green chilli into small pieces.
  5. Take the soaked moong dal in a large mixing bowl.
  6. Add chopped onion, tomato, green chilli and grated carrot.
  7. Add roasted peanuts, chaat masala, chilli powder and salt.
  8. Mix all the ingredients well.
  9. Add lemon juice and chopped coriander leaves.
  10. Mix everything gently and add sev on top.
  11. Serve immediately and enjoy your Moong Dal Masala!

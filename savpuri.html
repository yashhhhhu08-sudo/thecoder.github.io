sevPuri

Ingredients

  • Flat puris (Papdi) - 20
  • Boiled potatoes - 2
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Sev - 1 cup
  • Tamarind chutney - 3 tablespoons
  • Green chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Salt - As needed
  • Lemon juice - 1 tablespoon

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Chop the onion and tomato into small pieces.
  3. Arrange the flat puris on a serving plate.
  4. Place boiled potato pieces on each puri.
  5. Add chopped onion and tomato over the puris.
  6. Pour tamarind chutney and green chutney over the puris.
  7. Sprinkle chaat masala, chilli powder and salt.
  8. Add a generous amount of sev on top.
  9. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves.
  10. Add lemon juice for extra taste.
  11. Serve immediately and enjoy your Sev Puri!

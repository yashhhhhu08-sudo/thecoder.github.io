Pani Puri

Ingredients

  • Pani puri shells - 20
  • Boiled potatoes - 2
  • Cooked chickpeas - 1 cup
  • Mint leaves - 1 handful
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Tamarind chutney - 3 tablespoons
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Water - 3 cups

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes and mash them in a bowl.
  2. Add cooked chickpeas, salt and chaat masala.
  3. Grind mint leaves, coriander leaves and green chilli.
  4. Mix the ground paste with water to prepare the pani.
  5. Add tamarind chutney and salt to the pani.
  6. Make a small hole in each puri.
  7. Fill the puri with potato and chickpea mixture.
  8. Add prepared pani and serve immediately.

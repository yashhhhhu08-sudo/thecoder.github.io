Roti

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Water as needed
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Ghee or butter for serving

Instructions

  1. Add wheat flour and salt to a large mixing bowl.
  2. Gradually add water and knead until you get a soft and smooth dough.
  3. Add 1 teaspoon of oil and knead the dough for another 1–2 minutes.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into small equal-sized balls.
  6. Dust a dough ball with a little flour and roll it into a thin, round roti.
  7. Heat a tawa over medium-high heat.
  8. Place the rolled roti on the hot tawa. Cook until small bubbles appear.
  9. Flip the roti and cook the other side until light brown spots appear.
  10. Cook directly over a flame for a few seconds if desired, until the roti puffs up.
  11. Remove from the heat and apply a little ghee or butter.
  12. Serve the hot roti with vegetable curry, dal, paneer, or your favorite side dish.

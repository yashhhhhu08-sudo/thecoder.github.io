Homemade Apple Pie

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cold butter
  • 1/4 cup cold water
  • 5–6 apples, peeled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg powder
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, for brushing

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Mix flour and cold butter until the mixture becomes crumbly. Add cold water and form a dough.
  3. Wrap the dough and refrigerate it for about 30 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, combine sliced apples, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, lemon juice, and flour.
  5. Roll out half of the dough and place it into a pie dish.
  6. Add the prepared apple filling evenly over the crust.
  7. Roll out the remaining dough and cut it into strips. Arrange the strips in a lattice pattern over the apples.
  8. Brush the top with beaten egg.
  9. Bake for 45–55 minutes, or until the crust becomes golden brown and the apples are tender.
  10. Allow the apple pie to cool before slicing.
  11. Serve and enjoy your delicious homemade apple pie!

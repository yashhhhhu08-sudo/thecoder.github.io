Bisi Bele Bhat

Ingredients

  1. For the Rice and Lentils
    • 1 cup Sona Masuri rice or raw rice
    • ¾ cup toor dal (pigeon pea lentils)
    • ¼ cup raw peanuts (optional)
    • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
    • 4.5 to 5 cups water (for cooking soft and mushy)
    • Salt to taste
  2. For the Vegetables and Flavor Base
    • 2 cups mixed chopped vegetables (carrot, French beans, green peas, potato, and small brinjals)
    • 1 medium onion, chopped
    • 1 medium tomato, chopped
    • 1 tablespoon tightly packed tamarind (soaked in ½ cup warm water to extract pulp)
    • 3 to 4 tablespoons Bisi Bele Bath powder/masala
    • 1 teaspoon jaggery powder
    • 2 tablespoons ghee or oil
  3. For the Tempering (Tadak)
    • 2 tablespoons ghee
    • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
    • 10 to 15 cashews1 pinch hing (asafoetida)
    • 1 sprig curry leaves
    • 1 to 2 dried red chilies

    Instructions

    1. Soak and Cook Rice & Dal: Rinse the rice and peanuts (if using), and soak them in water for 20 minutes. In a pressure cooker, cook the rinsed toor dal with 2 cups of water and turmeric for 8 to 9 whistles until soft and mushy, then mash well. Cook the soaked rice and peanuts separately with 2.5 cups of water until very tender.
    2. Extract Tamarind: Soak the tamarind in warm water for 20 minutes, squeeze out the pulp, strain the liquid, and set aside.
    3. Cook Vegetables: In a large pot or pan, add the chopped vegetables, onion, tomato, and 2 cups of water. Cook until the vegetables turn tender.
    4. Add Spices and Tang: Stir the Bisi Bele Bath powder (mixed in a little water), tamarind extract, jaggery, and salt into the cooked vegetables. Simmer for 5 minutes to remove the raw smell of the spice powder.
    5. Combine Everything: Add the cooked rice-peanut mixture and the mashed toor dal into the vegetable and spice base. Mix thoroughly, adding 1 to 2 cups of hot water to achieve a porridge-like, smooth consistency. Simmer for 3 to 5 minutes.
    6. Prepare Tempering: Heat ghee in a small pan, splutter the mustard seeds, then fry the cashews, dried red chili, curry leaves, and hing until golden. Pour this hot tempering over the Bisi Bele Bath.
    7. Rest and Serve: Cover the pot for 5 minutes to let the flavors infuse, then serve hot with a generous extra drizzle of ghee and crispy papads or boondi.

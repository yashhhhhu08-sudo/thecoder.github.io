Paddu

Ingredients

  • For the Base
    •
  • 2 to 3 cups fermented idli or dosa batter
  • ½ cup finely chopped onions
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped1 tbsp fresh coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp curry leaves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp grated carrot (optional)
  • Salt to taste (for the vegetables)
  • Oil or ghee for greasing and cooking
  • For the Tempering (Oggarane)
    •
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 tsp chana dal (split Bengal gram)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Tempering: Heat a small pan with 1 tablespoon of oil. Add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and chana dal. Let them splutter and turn light golden.
  2. Sauté the Aromatics: Add the chopped green chilies, curry leaves, and finely chopped onions. Sauté until the onions turn translucent. Turn off the heat and let the mixture cool.
  3. Mix the Batter: Pour the cooled tempering into your dosa or idli batter. Add the chopped coriander leaves, grated carrots, and a pinch of salt if needed. Mix everything well.
  4. Heat the Paddu Pan: Place a special paddu pan (appe/kuzhi paniyaram pan) on medium heat. Add a few drops of oil or ghee into each cavity.
  5. Pour and Cook: Pour a spoonful of the prepared batter into each hole, filling them about three-quarters full. Cover the pan with a lid and let it cook on a medium-low flame for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom turns golden and crisp.
  6. Flip: Gently flip each paddu using a skewer or a small spoon. Add a few drops of oil around the edges and cook uncovered for another 1 to 2 minutes until both sides are evenly browned.
  7. Serve: Remove the hot paddu from the pan and serve them with coconut chutney or spicy tomato chutney

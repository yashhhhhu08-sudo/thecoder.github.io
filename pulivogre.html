Puliyogare

Ingredients

  1. For the Rice
    • Cooked rice: 2 to 3 cups (cooled completely, grains separated; Sona Masuri works best)
    • Sesame oil (or cooking oil): 1–2 teaspoons
    • Turmeric powder: 1/4 teaspoon
    • Salt: To taste
  2. For Puliyogare Spice Powder (Podi)
    • Coriander seeds: 2 teaspoons
    • Chana dal (split gram): 2 teaspoons
    • Urad dal (black gram): 2 teaspoons
    • Dried red chillies: 4 to 6 (adjust for spice)
    • Black peppercorns: 1/2 teaspoon
    • Fenugreek seeds (methi): 1/4 teaspoon
    • White or black sesame seeds: 1 teaspoon
  3. For Tamarind Paste (Gojju & Tempering)
    • Tamarind:Lemon-sized piece (soaked in 1/2 cup warm water and extracted to thick juice)
    • Oil/Sesame oil: 3 tablespoons
    • Mustard seeds: 1 teaspoon
    • Chana dal & Urad dal: 1 tablespoon each
    • Peanuts: 1/4 to 1/2 cup
    • Dried red chillies: 2
    • Curry leaves: 1 sprig
    • Asafoetida (hing): 1/4 teaspoon
    • Jaggery: 1 to 2 tablespoons (grated, to balance sourness)
    • Turmeric powder: 1/2 teaspoon
    • Salt: To taste

Instructions

  1. Cook and Cool the Rice
    • Wash and cook the rice so that the grains remain separate and firm, not mushy.
    • Spread the cooked rice on a wide plate, drizzle with 1–2 teaspoons of oil and a pinch of turmeric, and let it cool completely.
  2. Make the Spice Powder
    • Heat a small pan on low heat without oil.
    • Dry roast the coriander seeds, chana dal, urad dal, dried red chillies, peppercorns, fenugreek seeds, and sesame seeds until fragrant and slightly browned.
    • Let them cool down, then grind them into a coarse powder. Set aside.
  3. Prepare the Tamarind Gojju
    • Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan or kadai over medium heat.
    • Add mustard seeds and let them pop, then add the chana dal, urad dal, and peanuts. Fry until the nuts turn crunchy and golden.
    • Add dried red chillies, curry leaves, and asafoetida (hing).
    • Pour in the extracted tamarind juice, turmeric powder, jaggery, and salt.
    • Bring the mixture to a boil and let it simmer until it thickens and oil begins to separate from the edges.
    • Stir in the prepared Puliyogare spice powder, mix well, and cook for another minute before turning off the heat. Allow the gojju to cool slightly.
  4. Mix Everything Together
    • Add the prepared tamarind gojju little by little to the cooled rice.
    • Gently mix using a fork or light hand movements to ensure every grain of rice is evenly coated without breaking the rice.
    • Taste and adjust salt if needed. For the best flavor, let the Puliyogare rest for 30 minutes before serving so the spices absorb fully.

Cabbage Cutlet

Ingredients

  • 2 cups finely chopped cabbage
  • 1 boiled potato
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons rice flour
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt as required
  • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Finely chop the cabbage and grate the carrot.
  2. Mash the boiled potato in a bowl.
  3. Add cabbage, carrot, breadcrumbs, rice flour and spices.
  4. Add salt and ginger-garlic paste and mix everything well.
  5. Make small round balls from the mixture and flatten them slightly.
  6. Coat the cutlets with sesame seeds.
  7. Heat oil in a pan and shallow-fry the cutlets and place them on a plate
  8. serve hot with tomato ketchup or your favourite chutney

Samosa Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Water as required
  • Oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and mash them.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add green chillies, ginger and green peas.
  4. Add mashed potatoes, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
  5. Mix well and cook the filling for a few minutes.
  6. Mix flour, salt and oil in a bowl. Add water and make a firm dough.
  7. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a thin circle.
  8. Cut the circle into two halves and make a cone shape.
  9. Fill the cone with the potato mixture and seal the edges.
  10. Heat oil and deep-fry the samosas until golden brown and crispy.
  11. Serve hot with green chutney or tomato sauce.

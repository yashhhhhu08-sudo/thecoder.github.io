🍩 Delicious Donuts

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • Chocolate or strawberry icing
  • Sprinkles for decoration

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla essence.
  3. Mix everything to form a soft dough.
  4. Roll the dough and cut it into donut shapes.
  5. Heat oil in a pan and carefully fry the donuts until golden brown.
  6. Allow the donuts to cool.
  7. Dip them in chocolate or strawberry icing.
  8. Add colorful sprinkles on top.
  9. Serve and enjoy the delicious donuts! 🍩

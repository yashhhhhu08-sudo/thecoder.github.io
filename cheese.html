Cheese cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 40 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 4 large eggs
  • 19 ounces cherry pie filling

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 325°F (163°C).
  2. Crush the graham crackers into fine crumbs. Add melted butter and mix well.
  3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan.
  4. Bake the crust for 10 minutes and let it cool.
  5. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, flour, and vanilla until smooth.
  6. Add the sour cream and mix until combined.
  7. Add the eggs one at a time and mix gently. Do not overmix.
  8. Pour the cheesecake mixture over the crust.
  9. Wrap the bottom of the pan with aluminum foil and place it in a larger roasting pan.
  10. Add hot water to the roasting pan until it reaches about halfway up the springform pan.
  11. Bake for about 1 hour and 10 minutes, until the edges are set and the center slightly jiggles.
  12. Turn off the oven and leave the cheesecake inside with the door slightly open until it cools.
  13. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  14. Spread cherry pie filling on top, slice, and serve.

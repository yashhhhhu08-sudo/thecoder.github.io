Chocolate Fudge Brownie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  3. In a bowl, mix the melted butter and sugar.
  4. Add the eggs and vanilla extract, then mix well.
  5. Add flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
  6. Mix gently until the batter is smooth.
  7. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  8. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan.
  9. Bake for 25–30 minutes.
  10. Allow the brownie to cool before cutting into pieces.
  11. Serve and enjoy your delicious fudge brownie!

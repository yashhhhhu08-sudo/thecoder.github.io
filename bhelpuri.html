Bhel Puri

Ingredients

  • Puffed rice (Murmura) - 2 cups
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Boiled potato - 1
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Sev - ½ cup
  • Roasted peanuts - ½ cup
  • Tamarind chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Green chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Lemon juice - 1 tablespoon

Instructions

  1. Take puffed rice in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Chop the onion, tomato and green chilli into small pieces.
  3. Boil the potato, peel it and cut it into small pieces.
  4. Add chopped onion, tomato and potato to the puffed rice.
  5. Add roasted peanuts and sev.
  6. Pour tamarind chutney and green chutney into the bowl.
  7. Add chaat masala and salt as needed.
  8. Mix all the ingredients gently.
  9. Add lemon juice and chopped coriander leaves.
  10. Mix well and serve immediately.

Masala Puri

Ingredients

  • Dry peas - 1 cup
  • Small puri shells - 20
  • Potatoes - 2
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Ginger - 1 small piece
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - 1 teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Water - 3 cups

Instructions

  1. Soak the dry peas in water overnight.
  2. Cook the soaked peas in a pressure cooker until soft.
  3. Boil the potatoes and mash them.
  4. Grind ginger, green chilli and coriander leaves.
  5. Heat a pan and add the ground paste.
  6. Add cooked peas, chilli powder, chaat masala and salt.
  7. Add water and cook the masala until it becomes thick.
  8. Crush the puris and place them in a serving bowl.
  9. Pour the prepared masala over the crushed puris.
  10. Add chopped onion, tomato and coriander leaves.
  11. Serve the masala puri hot and enjoy!

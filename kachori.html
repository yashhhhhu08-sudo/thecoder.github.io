Kachori

Ingredients

  • Round kachori - 6
  • Boiled potatoes - 2
  • Cooked chickpeas - 1 cup
  • Curd (Yogurt) - 1 cup
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Sev - ½ cup
  • Tamarind chutney - 3 tablespoons
  • Green chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Salt - As needed

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Cook the chickpeas until they become soft.
  3. Whisk the curd in a bowl until smooth.
  4. Chop the onion and tomato into small pieces.
  5. Place the kachoris on a serving plate.
  6. Make a small hole in each kachori.
  7. Fill the kachori with boiled potatoes and cooked chickpeas.
  8. Add whisked curd over the kachoris.
  9. Pour tamarind chutney and green chutney over them.
  10. Sprinkle chaat masala, chilli powder and salt.
  11. Add sev and chopped coriander leaves.
  12. Serve immediately and enjoy your Kachori Chaat!

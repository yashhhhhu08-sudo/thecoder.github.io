Palav

Ingredients

  • Rice: 1 cup Sona Masuri or Basmati rice (soaked for 20 minutes and drained)
  • Ghee or Oil: 2 tablespoons
  • Whole Spices: 1 bay leaf, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 3 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1 star anise
  • Vegetables: ½ cup chopped beans, 1 small carrot (chopped), ½ cup green peas, 1 small potato (cubed), and a few cauliflower florets
  • Aromatics & Tomato: 1 medium onion (sliced) and 1 small tomato (chopped)
  • Fresh Herb & Spice Paste: Blend 4 garlic cloves, 1-inch ginger, 2 green chilies, a handful of mint leaves, and a handful of coriander leaves into a smooth paste.
  • Water & Salt: 2 cups hot water and salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Soak the Rice: Rinse the rice until water runs clear, soak for 20 minutes, and drain completely.
  2. Sauté Spices & Onions: Heat ghee or oil in a pressure cooker or heavy-bottom pan. Add the whole spices and let them crackle. Add the sliced onions and sauté until translucent.
  3. Add Veggies & Masala: Add the chopped vegetables and tomato. Sauté for 2 minutes. Pour in the ground ginger-garlic-herb paste and cook until the raw smell goes away.
  4. Add Rice & Water: Add the soaked, drained rice and gently mix it with the vegetables and spices. Pour in 2 cups of hot water and add salt to taste.
  5. Cook: Cover and pressure cook for 2 whistles on medium heat (or cover with a tight lid and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes if using a regular pan).
  6. Serve: Let the pressure release naturally, fluff the rice gently with a fork, and serve hot.

noodels

Ingredients

  • 200 grams noodles
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded
  • 1/2 cup capsicum, thinly sliced
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon chili sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add a little salt.
  2. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Drain the noodles and rinse them with cold water. Add a little oil and gently mix to prevent them from sticking.
  4. Heat oil in a large pan or wok over high heat.
  5. Add chopped garlic and ginger. Stir-fry for about 30 seconds.
  6. Add sliced onions, carrots, cabbage, and capsicum. Stir-fry the vegetables for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Add soy sauce, vinegar, chili sauce, black pepper, and salt. Mix well.
  8. Add the cooked noodles to the pan.
  9. Toss everything together on high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  10. Add chopped spring onions and mix well.
  11. Turn off the heat and serve the noodles hot.

Chana Chaat

Ingredients

  • 1 cup boiled chickpeas (chana)
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 small cucumber, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons pomegranate seeds
  • 2 tablespoons peanuts
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon chaat masala
  • 1/4 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the chickpeas until they become soft and drain the water.
  2. Take the boiled chickpeas in a large bowl.
  3. Add chopped onion, tomato, cucumber and green chilli.
  4. Add peanuts and pomegranate seeds.
  5. Add coriander leaves, chaat masala, red chilli powder and salt.
  6. Add lemon juice and mix all the ingredients well.
  7. Serve the chana chaat fresh and enjoy.

Rice and sambar

Ingredients

  • Rice - 1 cup
  • Toor Dal - 1/2 cup
  • Vegetables - 1 cup
  • Sambar Powder - 2 spoons
  • Salt - as required
  • Oil - 1 spoon

Instructions

  1. Wash and cook the rice.
  2. Cook the dal and vegetables.
  3. Add sambar powder and salt.
  4. Boil for 10 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with rice.

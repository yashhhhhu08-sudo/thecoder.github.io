Alootakki

Ingredients

  • Boiled potatoes - 4
  • Corn flour - 2 tablespoons
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Red chilli powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Garam masala - ½ teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Oil - 2 tablespoons
  • Curd (Yogurt) - ½ cup
  • Tamarind chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Green chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Sev - ½ cup

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and mash them in a bowl.
  2. Add corn flour, chopped green chilli and coriander leaves.
  3. Add red chilli powder, chaat masala, garam masala and salt.
  4. Mix all the ingredients well to prepare the tikki mixture.
  5. Divide the mixture into small portions.
  6. Shape each portion into a round, flat tikki.
  7. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  8. Place the tikkis in the pan and cook until golden brown on both sides.
  9. Place the cooked tikkis on a serving plate.
  10. Add curd, tamarind chutney and green chutney over the tikkis.
  11. Sprinkle chaat masala and sev on top.
  12. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

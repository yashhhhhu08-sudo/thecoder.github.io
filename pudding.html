Crème Caramel Pudding

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sugar – for caramel
  • 2 cups milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Heat sugar in a pan over medium heat until it melts and turns golden brown.
  2. Pour the caramel into a pudding mould and spread it evenly.
  3. Heat the milk gently without boiling it.
  4. In a bowl, whisk the eggs and sugar until well combined.
  5. Add the warm milk and vanilla extract. Mix gently.
  6. Strain the mixture and pour it into the caramel-coated mould.
  7. Cover the mould and steam for about 25–30 minutes, or until set.
  8. Allow the pudding to cool completely.
  9. Refrigerate for 2–3 hours.
  10. Run a knife around the edges and carefully invert the pudding onto a plate.
  11. Serve chilled and enjoy!

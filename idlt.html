Idle

Ingredients

  • Idli rice (parboiled rice): 3 cups
  • Urad dal (split white black gram): 1 cup
  • Fenugreek seeds (methi): ½ teaspoon (aids fermentation)
  • Thick poha (flattened rice): ¼ cup (optional, for extra softness)
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)
  • Water: As needed for soaking and grinding
  • Oil or Ghee: For greasing moulds

Instructions

  1. Soak
    • Rinse idli rice and poha together 2–3 times. Soak in fresh water for 4–6 hours.
    • Rinse urad dal and fenugreek seeds together. Soak in a separate bowl of fresh water for 4–6 hours.
  2. Grind
    • Drain the urad dal and methi seeds. Grind with small batches of water into a smooth, fluffy batter. Transfer to a large pot.
    • Drain the rice and poha. Grind with a little water into a slightly coarse, semolina-like texture. Add to the dal batter.
  3. Ferment
    • Add salt and mix the batter gently with clean hands (hand warmth helps start fermentation).
    • Cover the pot with a lid. Leave it in a warm spot for 8–12 hours until the batter rises and looks puffy.
  4. Steam
    • Stir the fermented batter lightly (do not deflate completely).
    • Grease your idli plate moulds with oil or ghee.
    • Fill each mould about ¾ full with batter.
    • Heat water in an idli steamer. Place the stand inside once water boils.
    • Steam on medium-high heat for 10–12 minutes.
    • Cool for 5 minutes, then remove idlis using a wet spoon

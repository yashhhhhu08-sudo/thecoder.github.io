Dosa

Ingredients

  • 3 boiled or 1 raw medium potato (chopped or roughly mashed)
  • 1½ cup rice flour
  • ¼ cup rava (semolina / suji)
  • 1 to 5 cups water (adjusted for a watery batter)
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 2 to 3 green chilies
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Few curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for roasting

Instructions

  1. Blend the potato: If using a raw potato, chop it and wash it in water to remove starch. Blend the potato with a little water into a smooth paste.
  2. Mix the batter: Transfer the potato paste to a large bowl. Add the rice flour, rava, salt, and water. Whisk well until the batter has a thin, watery consistency without lumps.
  3. Add aromatics: Stir in the chopped onion, green chilies, cumin seeds, curry leaves, and coriander leaves.
  4. Rest the batter: Let the mixture rest for 10 minutes so the rava absorbs the moisture.
  5. Cook the dosa: Heat a non-stick pan until it is super hot. Pour a ladleful of the watery batter starting from the edges toward the center.
  6. Roast until crisp: Drizzle 1 tsp of oil around the edges. Cook on medium-high heat for 2 minutes until the bottom turns golden and crisp. Fold and serve hot.

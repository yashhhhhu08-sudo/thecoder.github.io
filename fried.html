fried rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup capsicum, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper powder
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice and allow it to cool completely. For best results, use leftover refrigerated rice.
  2. Heat oil in a large wok or frying pan over high heat.
  3. Add chopped garlic and ginger. Stir-fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add chopped onion and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  5. Add carrots, green peas, capsicum, and cabbage. Stir-fry the vegetables for 3–4 minutes.
  6. Add the cooled cooked rice to the pan.
  7. Add soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and salt.
  8. Toss everything together on high heat for 2–3 minutes. Make sure the rice is mixed well with the vegetables.
  9. Add chopped spring onions and mix well.
  10. Turn off the heat and serve the fried rice hot.

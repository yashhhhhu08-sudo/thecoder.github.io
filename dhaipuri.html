Dhai Puri

Ingredients

  • Pani puri shells - 20
  • Boiled potatoes - 2
  • Cooked chickpeas - ½ cup
  • Curd (Yogurt) - 1 cup
  • Onion - 1
  • Sev - ½ cup
  • Tamarind chutney - 3 tablespoons
  • Green chutney - 2 tablespoons
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful
  • Salt - As needed

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes, peel them and mash them in a bowl.
  2. Cook the chickpeas until they become soft.
  3. Whisk the curd in a bowl until it becomes smooth.
  4. Chop the onion into small pieces.
  5. Make a small hole in each puri.
  6. Fill the puri with mashed potato and cooked chickpeas.
  7. Add whisked curd over the puris.
  8. Pour tamarind chutney and green chutney over the puris.
  9. Sprinkle chaat masala, chilli powder and salt.
  10. Add sev and chopped coriander leaves.
  11. Serve immediately and enjoy your Dahi Puri!

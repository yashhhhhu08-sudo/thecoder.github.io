Poori Sagu

Ingredients

  1. For the Masala Paste
    • Grated coconut: ½ cup
    • Roasted gram dal (hittu/hurigadale): 2 tablespoons
    • Cashew nuts: 4 to 5
    • Whole spices: 1-inch cinnamon, 3 cloves, 2 green cardamoms (optional)
    • Seeds: 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera), 1 teaspoon fennel seeds (saunf)
    • Aromatics & Greens: 1 inch ginger, 2 garlic cloves, 3-4 green chilies, a small handful of fresh coriander leaves, and a few mint leaves
    • Water: As required to grind into a smooth paste
  2. For the Sagu
    • Mixed vegetables: 1.5 cups total (chopped carrots, French beans, green peas, and potatoes)
    • Oil: 2 tablespoons
    • Tempering: 1 teaspoon mustard seeds, ½ teaspoon cumin seeds, a pinch of asafoetida (hing), and 10 curry leaves
    • Onion: 1 medium (finely sliced or chopped)
    • Tomato: 1 medium (chopped)
    • Turmeric powder: ¼ teaspoon
    • Salt: To taste
    • Water: 1.5 to 2 cups

Instructions

  1. Grind the Masala Paste
    • Combine the coconut, roasted gram dal, cashews, whole spices, cumin, fennel, ginger, garlic, green chilies, coriander, and mint in a blender jar.
    • Add a little water and grind everything into a smooth, fine paste. Set it aside.
  2. Cook the Vegetables
    • Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wide pan or kadai over medium heat.
    • Add the mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and a pinch of asafoetida. Let them splutter.
    • Add the curry leaves and chopped onions. Sauté until the onions turn soft and translucent.
    • Add the chopped tomatoes and sauté until soft and mushy.
    • Toss in the chopped mixed vegetables (carrots, beans, peas, and potatoes) along with ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder and salt.
    • Pour in 1 cup of water, cover the pan with a lid, and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  3. Combine and Simmer
    • Add the freshly ground coconut masala paste to the cooked vegetables.
    • Pour in another ½ cup to 1 cup of water to adjust the gravy consistency (sagu should be medium-thick, not too watery).
    • Mix well and let the mixture come to a rolling boil.
    • Simmer on low-to-medium heat for about 5 minutes so the raw smell of the spices disappears and the flavors meld together.
    • Turn off the heat. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves

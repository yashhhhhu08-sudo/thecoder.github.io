Vegetable Cutlet

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes
  • 1/2 cup carrots
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 cup beans
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt as required
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes and vegetables until they are soft.
  2. Mash the boiled potatoes and vegetables together.
  3. Add onion, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
  4. Mix everything well and make small round cutlets.
  5. Coat each cutlet with bread crumbs.
  6. Heat oil in a pan and shallow-fry the cutlets.
  7. Cook both sides until they become golden brown and crispy.
  8. Serve hot with tomato ketchup or chutney.

Chapati Vegcurry

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour - 2 cups
  • Water - as required
  • Mixed vegetables - 2 cups
  • Salt - as required
  • Oil - 1 spoon

Instructions

  1. Mix wheat flour with water.
  2. Prepare a soft dough.
  3. Roll the dough into chapathi.
  4. Cook on a hot pan.
  5. Prepare vegetable curry and serve.

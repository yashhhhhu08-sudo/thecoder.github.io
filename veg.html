Veg Biriyani

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 green cardamoms
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Boil 4 cups of water in a large pot. Add salt, bay leaf, cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves.
  3. Add the soaked rice and cook until it is about 70–80% cooked. Drain the rice and keep it aside.
  4. Heat oil and ghee in a large pan or heavy-bottomed pot.
  5. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  6. Add sliced onions and green chilies. Fry until the onions become golden brown.
  7. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 1–2 minutes.
  8. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  9. Add turmeric powder, chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix well.
  10. Add carrots, potatoes, beans, and green peas. Cook for 5–7 minutes.
  11. Add yogurt and mix everything well. Cook until the vegetables are almost tender.
  12. Add half of the cooked rice over the vegetable mixture.
  13. Sprinkle some coriander leaves and mint leaves over the rice.
  14. Add the remaining rice and garnish with the remaining coriander and mint leaves.
  15. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and cook on low heat for 10–15 minutes.
  16. Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest for 5 minutes.
  17. Gently mix the layers and serve hot.

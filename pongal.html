Pongal

Ingredients

  • Rice: ½ cup raw short-grain rice (like Sona Masuri or Ponni)
  • Moong Dal: ½ cup split yellow moong dal (split lentils)
  • Water: 3 cups (or up to 3¾ cups for a softer, mushier texture)
  • Ghee: 3 to 4 tablespoons (clarified butter)
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)

    For Tempering (Tadka)

  • Black Peppercorns: 1 to 2 teaspoons (coarsely crushed)
  • Cumin Seeds: 1 teaspoon
  • Ginger: 1 tablespoon, finely chopped or grated
  • Cashews: 10 to 12 pieces (halved)
  • Curry Leaves: 10 to 12 fresh leaves
  • Asafoetida (Hing): A small pinch
  • Green Chili: 1 (slit, optional)

Instructions

  1. Roast and Wash the Grains
    • Dry roast the moong dal in a small pan on medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes until it smells fragrant and turns light golden.
    • Rinse the roasted moong dal and raw rice together thoroughly in water.
  2. Pressure Cook
    • Transfer the washed rice and dal into a pressure cooker.
    • Add 3 to 3¾ cups of water and salt.
    • Cover and cook on medium flame for 4 to 5 whistles (until the grains are completely soft and mushy).
    • Let the pressure release naturally. Open the lid and mash the mixture gently with a spoon or ladle.
  3. Prepare the Tempering
    • Heat 2 tablespoons of ghee in a separate small pan.
    • Fry the cashews until golden brown.
    • Add the cumin seeds, crushed black peppercorns, ginger, curry leaves, slit green chili, and asafoetida.
    • Sauté the spices in the hot ghee for about 30 seconds until aromatic.
  4. Combine and Serve
    • Pour the hot ghee tempering over the cooked rice and dal mixture.
    • Mix everything well. If the consistency feels too thick, add a splash of warm water and mix over low heat.
    • Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

🍨 Ice Cream

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • Strawberries for decoration

Instructions

  1. Mix the milk, sugar and cocoa powder in a bowl.
  2. Add heavy cream and vanilla essence.
  3. Whisk the mixture until it becomes smooth.
  4. Add chocolate chips and mix well.
  5. Pour the mixture into a container.
  6. Cover and freeze for 6–8 hours.
  7. Scoop the ice cream into a bowl.
  8. serve with chocolate sauce and strawberries
  9. serve chilled and enjoy!

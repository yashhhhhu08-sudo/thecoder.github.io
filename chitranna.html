Chitranna

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice: 3 to 4 cups (fluffy, cooled, non-sticky; leftover works best)
  • Lemon juice: 1.5 to 2 tablespoons (freshly squeezed)
  • Oil: 2 to 3 tablespoons (sesame/gingelly or neutral oil; coconut oil optional)
  • Peanuts: 3 tablespoons (raw or roasted)
  • Chana dal: 1 tablespoon (split bengal gram)
  • Urad dal: 1 teaspoon (split black gram)
  • Mustard seeds: 1 teaspoon
  • Green chilies: 1 to 2 (finely chopped)
  • Dry red chilies: 1 to 2 (broken)
  • Curry leaves: 10 to 15 leaves
  • Turmeric powder: ¼ to ½ teaspoon (for yellow color)
  • Asafoetida (hing): 1 generous pinch
  • Grated coconut: 2 to 3 tablespoons (optional, traditional in some regional variants)
  • Cashew nuts: 1 tablespoon (optional)
  • Salt: To taste
  • Coriander leaves: Chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the rice: cooked rice on a wide plate and let it cool completely so grains stay separate.
  2. Temper the base: Heat oil in a wide pan or kadai on medium heat.
  3. Fry nuts and lentils: Add peanuts and fry until crunchy (about 1 minute). Add chana dal and urad dal; cook until light golden brown.
  4. Add arosmatics: Toss in mustard seeds, cumin seeds, dry red chilies, green chilies, and curry leaves. Let mustard seeds crackle.
  5. Add spice and turn off heat: Stir in turmeric powder and asafoetida. Turn off the stove or lower flame to minimum so spices do not burn.
  6. Combine tempering and rice: Pour the warm tempering over the cooled rice, or add rice directly into the pan. Add salt and lemon juice.
  7. Mix gently: Toss everything thoroughly on low flame or off heat until the yellow color and tang distribute evenly.
  8. Rest and serve: Garnish with coriander leaves. Let it rest for 15–30 minutes before serving

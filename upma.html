Upma

Ingredients

  • Rava or suji:1 cup semolina, 2 cups boiling hot water, and 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Spices and lentils: 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tbsp urad dal, and 1 tbsp chana dal
  • Aromatics: 1 small onion, 1-2 green chilies, grated ginger, and 8–10 curry leaves
  • Seasoning and garnish: Salt, 1 tbsp lemon juice (optional), and chopped fresh coriander leaves

Instructions

  1. Roast the Rava:Dry roast 1 cup of semolina on medium-low heat for 3–5 minutes until fragrant and light golden, then set aside to cool.
  2. Make the Tempering: Heat oil or ghee, splutter mustard seeds, and fry the cumin seeds, urad dal, and chana dal until golden brown.
  3. Sauté Aromatics:Add green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, and onions, sautéing until translucent.
  4. Boil the Water: Add salt and 2 cups of water, bringing it to a rolling boil.
  5. Add Rava: Lower the heat and gradually stir in the roasted semolina continuously to avoid lumps.
  6. Simmer and Cover:Once the water is absorbed, cover and cook on low heat for 3 to 5 minutes.
  7. Garnish and Serve: Turn off the heat, mix in lemon juice, garnish with coriander leaves, and serve hot.

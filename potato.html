Veggie Potato Sticks

Ingredients

  • 3 boiled potatoes
  • 1/2 cup boiled peas
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • 2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons corn flour
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Mash the boiled potatoes in a bowl.
  2. Add boiled peas, grated carrot and chopped green chillies.
  3. Add chilli powder, garam masala, cumin powder and salt.
  4. Mix everything well to make a soft mixture.
  5. Add corn flour and breadcrumbs and mix again.
  6. Take small portions of the mixture and shape them into sticks.
  7. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  8. Deep fry the potato sticks until golden and crispy.
  9. Remove them and place on a paper towel to remove excess oil.
  10. Serve hot with tomato sauce or your favourite chutney.

Aloo Paratha

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta), plus extra for dusting
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon oil or ghee, plus extra for cooking
  • 3/4 cup warm water (add gradually)
  • Ghee or oil for layering and toasting

Instructions

  1. Make the Dough
    • Mix the whole wheat flour and salt in a large bowl.
    • Add 1 teaspoon of oil and mix it into the flour.
    • Pour in warm water little by little and knead until you get a soft, smooth, and pliable dough.
    • Cover the dough and let it rest for 15 to 20 minutes.
    • Divide the dough into 6 equal lemon-sized balls.
  2. Roll and Layer (Triangle Method)
    • Take one dough ball, flatten it slightly, and dust it with dry flour.
    • Roll it out into a flat circle about 6 inches wide.
    • Spread a few drops of oil or ghee over the surface and sprinkle a tiny pinch of dry flour.
    • Fold the circle in half to form a semi-circle.
    • Add a few more drops of oil or ghee and a pinch of flour on the folded side.
    • Fold it again to form a neat triangle, then gently roll it out once more into a triangular paratha
  3. Cook the Paratha
    • Heat a flat pan or tawa over medium heat.
    • Place the rolled paratha onto the hot pan.
    • Cook until small bubbles or opaque spots appear on the surface, then flip it over.
    • Spread a little oil or ghee on the cooked side.
    • Flip again and press gently with a spatula, applying ghee or oil to the other side until both sides have golden-brown spots and look crispy.
    • Remove from the pan and serve hot

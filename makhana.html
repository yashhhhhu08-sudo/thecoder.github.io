Makhana Masala

Ingredients

  • Makhana (Fox nuts) - 2 cups
  • Oil or ghee - 2 tablespoons
  • Onion - 1
  • Tomato - 1
  • Green chilli - 1
  • Ginger-garlic paste - 1 teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder - ½ teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder - 1 teaspoon
  • Garam masala - ½ teaspoon
  • Chaat masala - 1 teaspoon
  • Salt - As needed
  • Coriander leaves - 1 handful

Instructions

  1. Heat a pan and add oil or ghee.
  2. Add makhana and roast them on low flame until crispy.
  3. Remove the roasted makhana and keep them aside.
  4. Chop the onion, tomato and green chilli into small pieces.
  5. Heat a little oil in the pan and add chopped onion.
  6. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a few minutes.
  7. Add chopped tomato and cook until soft.
  8. Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
  9. Mix all the ingredients well.
  10. Add the roasted makhana to the pan.
  11. Mix gently until the makhana is coated with the masala.
  12. Sprinkle chaat masala and chopped coriander leaves.
  13. Serve immediately and enjoy your Makhana Masala!
